About Our Bakery

Since 2018 • Karachi

Baked with Love, Served with Happiness

At Sweet Crumbs Bakery, we believe that life's most precious memories are celebrated around a cake box and warm pastries shared with loved ones.

What started as an intimate passion project in Clifton, Karachi has grown into a beloved benchmark of fine patisserie. We refuse shortcuts: we never use margarine, artificial fondants, or chemical stabilizers. Instead, our master bakers fold slow-fermented doughs, simmer fruit compotes from scratch, and whip fresh farm cream every morning.

Whether it is our signature Belgian Chocolate Fudge Cake, our 27-layer crisp butter croissants, or an ornate multi-tiered wedding masterpiece, our mission remains steadfast: to serve authentic happiness with every delicate bite.
