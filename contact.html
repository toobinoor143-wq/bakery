Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How early should I order a custom cake?

For standard celebration cakes, we recommend 24-48 hours. For intricate multi-tiered wedding cakes, please book at least 5-7 days in advance.

What are your delivery areas?

We deliver across Clifton, DHA, PECHS, Gulshan, and Bahadurabad. Free delivery is provided on orders over PKR 2,500.

Do you accept Cash on Delivery?

Yes! Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for all standard bakery items. For large custom tiered wedding cakes, a partial online deposit is required.

Are your ingredients 100% Halal?

Absolutely. All gelatins are 100% Halal beef-certified, all vanilla flavorings are non-alcoholic, and we only use pure Normandy dairy butter.
