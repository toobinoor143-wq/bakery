Our Heritage & Passion

Baked with Love, Served with Happiness

Founded in 2018 in Karachi, Sweet Crumbs Bakery was born from a simple devotion: to revive honest, small-batch European baking traditions using pure, uncompromised ingredients.

Every morning at 4:00 AM, our ovens come alive. We never use artificial preservatives, chemical premixes, or vegetable shortenings. Instead, we knead with 100% Normandy butter, farm-fresh eggs, raw cane sugars, and authentic Belgian couvertures. For us, every crumb tells a story of care and warmth.
